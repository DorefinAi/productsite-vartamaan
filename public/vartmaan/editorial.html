What Vartmaan AI is for, what it refuses to do, and what is not live yet.

Editorial

Read-only by design

A desk tool earns trust by what it refuses to do.

A newsroom's footage is its record. Reporters put it on the FTP under names they chose, in folders the video desk knows by heart. A tool that changes those files, however helpfully, breaks something people rely on at the worst moment: when a story is breaking.

So Vartmaan AI reads the reporter's footage and leaves it as it is. It never changes the reporter's footage; the only place it writes is its own folder, and there the desk can rename or move Vartmaan's own story folders. When it cannot be sure which folder holds a story's footage, it says so, with Waiting or Not found, and lets a person pick the folder by hand.

The software does the fetching and the matching. People make the calls.

The same rule runs through the whole desk. Vartmaan does not write stories and does not approve them. It gathers what reporters send, keeps their slug as they typed it, tags the district and puts the footage beside the text.

A person on the desk edits, approves or rejects every story before it reaches Octopus. A FLASH goes out because someone pressed Approve, and a KILL comes back because someone decided it should.

That restraint is deliberate, and it is why some work is still on the bench. Each of the features in the box beside this column will go live when it meets the same standard, and not before.

Corrections and clarifications

Things people often assume about Vartmaan AI, set straight.

  • The FTP is not a filing channel.Reporters file on WhatsApp, in Outlook or through the Reporter app. They upload footage to the FTP, and Vartmaan finds it there.
  • Vartmaan does not draft stories.The text is the reporter's and the edits are the desk's, made in the story editor.
  • Nothing is sent on its own.Every entry in the feed for Octopus is there because a person on the desk approved it.
  • The slug is not rewritten.It is kept as typed. A repeat slug gets _2 at the end so two stories never share a name.
  • Hold, Send back and Duplicate are not live.They are listed under Coming next, with the other planned work.
