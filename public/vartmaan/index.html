For the input desk

Every reporter's file reaches one desk, with its slug and its footage. People make every call.

From the assignment desk / Jaipur

Stories arrive on WhatsApp, in Outlook and through the Reporter app. Vartmaan AI gathers them into one inbox, keeps the reporter's slug as typed, tags the district, and finds the footage the reporter already put on the newsroom FTP.

It never changes the reporter's footage; the only place it writes is its own folder. Nothing reaches Octopus, the newsroom system, until a person on the desk approves it.

How a reporter's story reaches the desk with its footage

VARTMAAN AI 00:00.0 / 00:21.6

  1. 00:00 / STORY FILED

    A reporter files a story. The slug 141026_UDR_PARYATAN_SEASON_AV_REPORTER is kept as typed and read as Udaipur, tourist season, AV.

  2. 00:05 / VARTMAAN AI

    Vartmaan AI: assignment-desk intake for newsrooms.

  3. 00:08 / ONE INBOX

    WhatsApp, Outlook and the Reporter app go into one inbox. Pieces sent within 90 seconds become one item, and mail is checked every 60 seconds.

  4. 00:13 / FOOTAGE FOUND

    It finds the footage the reporter already put on the FTP. It scans every 5 minutes and waits up to 4 hours.

  5. 00:16 / READ-ONLY

    Read-only on the reporter's footage: it never changes the reporter's footage; the only place it writes is its own folder.

  6. 00:18 / DESK DECIDES

    The desk edits, rejects or approves. The story moves along one line: Received, Media, Review, FTP folder, RSS feed.

  7. 00:20 / FEED FOR OCTOPUS

    Approved stories reach Octopus as a feed, with FLASH, UPDATE and KILL.
